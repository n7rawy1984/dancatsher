## Design decision

Petroleum/cyan/gold editorial corporate design; authentic site photography, precise rules and strong bilingual type. Hero pairs substantial engineering messaging with an architectural photo. Seven catalog categories have a featured pipes/fittings panel and a compact index. Homepage shows examples of site work, not an asserted delivered-project portfolio. No invented clients, approvals, ratings, years/projects delivered, specifications, partnerships or testimonials.

Reference site was requested read-only but could not be retrieved. Its described navigation clarity informs structure; no visual or technical clone.
